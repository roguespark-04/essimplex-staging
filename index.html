Product · Nody Bot

Nody Bot

Not dead yet.

Battery-powered Ethernet phone-home for datacenter construction. It hangs on the far end of a cable run and wakes on a schedule to prove the drop is still alive, through months of cabling, without PoE and without staying pingable.

  • Outbound heartbeat only. Not ICMP-pingable; no always-on listener on an unfinished network.
  • Battery powered, not PoE. Works at the far end before power distribution is ready.
  • Configured over USB. Set up on the bench, then shipped to the install crew.

How Nody Bot works

One check-in cycle, repeated for the life of the build.

Built for multi-month cable plant builds, not always-on monitoring.

See the full sequence

  2. Wake

    A timer fires about every 15 minutes.

  3. Connect

    Wired Ethernet comes up and gets an address.

  4. Report

    A heartbeat goes to your collector.

  5. Confirm

    It waits for your collector to confirm.

  6. Sleep

    Back to sleep. Nothing listens in between.
