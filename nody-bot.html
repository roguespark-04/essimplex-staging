How it works

Sleep. Wake. Prove it. Sleep.

Built for multi-month cable plant builds, not always-on monitoring. One cycle, repeated for the life of the build.

  1. Wake
  2. Connect
  3. Report
  4. Confirm
  5. Sleep

  1. Step 1 of 5

    Wake

    A timer fires about every 15 minutes. Between wakes it sleeps with nothing listening, so the battery lasts the build schedule.

    ~15 min, configurable

  2. Step 2 of 5

    Connect

    Wired Ethernet comes up and the unit gets an address on the plant network.

    wired Ethernetno Wi-Fi dependency

  3. Step 3 of 5

    Report

    The unit sends a heartbeat to your collector so you know the drop is still alive.

    outbound only

  4. Step 4 of 5

    Confirm

    The unit waits for your collector to confirm the check-in, retrying if needed.

    confirmed check-in

  5. Step 5 of 5

    Sleep

    It returns to sleep until the next wake. Nothing listens in between, so it is never pingable.

    sleep

Why Nody Bot

Proof it’s alive when the plant isn’t finished.

During AI and HPC hall construction, cable drops sit unfinished for months. You need to know they still work, without leaving something pingable on an incomplete network and without PoE at the far end.

  • Not pingable by design

    Asleep between wakes. No ICMP, and no open ports waiting for scans on an unfinished plant.

  • Months on battery

    Battery power, not PoE, so the far end works before power distribution is ready.

  • Your collector

    Check-ins report to your collector. This site is marketing; collector and operations software ships separately.

Who it’s for

  • Datacenter construction and cable verification crews
  • Commissioning teams that need live confirmation across hundreds of drops
  • Integrators building AI and HPC halls on multi-month plant schedules
